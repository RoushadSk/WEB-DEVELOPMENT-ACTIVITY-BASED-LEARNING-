What Makes a Good Movie?

Story: A strong story keeps viewers interested from beginning to end.

Acting: Good performances make characters believable and memorable.

Music: Songs and background music create emotion and improve the viewing experience.

Direction: Good direction brings the story, actors and visuals together effectively.

Overall Rating: Ratings help viewers compare movies and decide what to watch next.
